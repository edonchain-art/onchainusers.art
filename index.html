The Protocol

Onchain Users is built on one core idea: art and identity can live entirely onchain, owned by the people who hold it, with no dependency on IPFS, a server, or any company staying in business.

Every image and every trait is generated directly from bytes stored in the smart contract itself. As long as Ethereum exists, so do the Users.

This is a collection for the people who are already here — the builders, the believers, and everyone riding the waves of a fully onchain future.

Onchain Users is released under CC0 — full public domain. No copyright, no gatekeeping, no permission needed. Holders and non-holders alike are free to build with the art, forever.

03 · Manifesto

Why We're
Onchain.

Onchain. Permanently.

Onchain Users exists for the people who are already here.

Crypto users scattered across every timezone. Builders, collectors, traders, degens, artists, researchers, and believers. People who have lived through the cycles that were supposed to break our conviction — and kept showing up.

Still building.
Still experimenting.
Still hodling.
Still here.

We are the builders shipping code nobody asked for.

We are the people who became fascinated by a technology before most people understood why it mattered.

We said "gm" to strangers before we knew their real names.

We learned to read block explorers. We learned what gas costs feel like. We learned that ownership could mean something different.

And somewhere along the way, we realized that hodl was never really about the price.

It was about staying.

It was about believing that the internet could be different.

We are worldwide users of the world computer.

The World Computer

"Ethereum is the world computer."

— Vitalik Buterin, Devcon 2024

To us, that's not a metaphor.

It's the premise.

A world computer that doesn't belong to a single company. A network that doesn't need permission to exist. A place where information, ownership, and value can persist without asking anyone for access.

That's why we're onchain.

Being onchain isn't a phase for us.

It's a philosophy.

It's catching the next meta before it has a name. Finding the next wave before it has a chart. Building things before there's a market for them.

We don't wait for the future to arrive. We're already there, refreshing the page.

Fully Onchain

Fully onchain isn't a feature.

It's the point.

Every piece in Onchain Users lives entirely on Ethereum. No IPFS. No external server. No database sitting behind a company that might disappear. No dependency on a platform staying alive.

The artwork, the data, the identity of each piece — all of it belongs to the chain.

It's made to last as long as Ethereum lasts.

That's not a technical flex.

That's the philosophy.

If something is truly onchain, it doesn't need to ask permission to survive.

CC0

That's also why the art is CC0.

No copyright.
No gatekeeping.
No asking permission to build on what we've built.

If the work is going to live onchain beyond any one person, company, or generation, then it shouldn't be locked away by one either.

Take it. Remix it. Build with it. Share it. Make it yours.

The chain is public. The art should be too.

Onchain Users

This isn't a collection for people waiting to see where Web3 goes.

It's for the people who are already here.

For the users. For the builders. For the believers. For everyone who decided that the internet could become something more permanent, more open, and more owned by the people who use it.

We didn't grow up onchain. We moved there. And we're not leaving.

Welcome to Onchain Users.

Not a phase. A lifestyle.
